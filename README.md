# 🌾 Interactive Agriculture Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Interactive Agriculture Analytics Dashboard** is a Power BI project designed to analyze the relationship between **rainfall patterns** and **agricultural productivity** across Indian states from **1966 to 2017**.

This dashboard helps identify rainfall trends, evaluate their impact on crop production, compare state-wise agricultural performance, and generate actionable insights to support data-driven agricultural planning and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Agriculture is highly dependent on climatic conditions, especially rainfall. Understanding the correlation between rainfall and crop productivity can help stakeholders:

* Improve crop planning and resource allocation
* Optimize irrigation strategies
* Reduce climate-related agricultural risks
* Increase overall agricultural productivity
* Support evidence-based decision making

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Rainfall demonstrates a moderate positive correlation with agricultural production.
* West Bengal emerged as one of the highest agricultural producing states.
* Bihar and Odisha consistently show strong agricultural performance.
* Peak rainfall occurs during the monsoon season, significantly influencing crop productivity.
* Several states achieve high productivity despite moderate rainfall, indicating efficient agricultural practices.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Improve irrigation infrastructure in low-rainfall regions.
* Promote precision agriculture and modern farming techniques.
* Utilize rainfall forecasting for better crop planning.
* Encourage efficient water management practices.
* Adopt successful agricultural practices from top-performing states.
